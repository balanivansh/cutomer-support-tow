## PART A: OVERVIEW

### A1. What is Tug of War?
**Audience:** Everyone
Tug of War is an interactive, Antakshari-style music challenge started by a Broadcaster during a live stream. Viewers join as participants and sing. The live audience votes to decide who wins. Prizes are paid in Gold Coins.

### A2. How a Tug of War match runs, step by step
**Audience:** Everyone
1. **Setup:** The Broadcaster starts the match and the prize pool is funded.
2. **Joining:** Viewers join the open seats.
3. **Matching:** Participants are paired head to head: 4 participants make 2 pairs and 6 participants make 3 pairs. The pairs sing one after the other.
4. **Voting:** After each pair finishes, the audience has 15 seconds to vote.
5. **Reveal:** After all pairs are done, the broadcaster sings a song while revealing the winners one by one.
6. **Completed:** Participant rewards are credited automatically and the match ends. The broadcaster's 9 coins are added to the daily live target (see B12).
A match can also be **Cancelled** (Broadcaster only, early stage) or **Paused** (see B7).

### A3. Do pairs get a challenge?
**Audience:** Participant
A pair may or may not be given a challenge. When it is, each singer gets a different keyword (a word) to sing about.

## PART B: BROADCASTER

### B1. How do I start a Tug of War?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
During your live stream, tap the **Piggy icon** and choose **Tug of War**. Then pick a match size and how to pay for it (free credit or coins).

### B2. What match sizes and costs are available?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
There are two fixed options: **4 participants for 20 Gold Coins** and **6 participants for 30 Gold Coins**.

### B3. How can I start a match: free credit or coins?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
There are two ways to start:
1. **Free credit:** if you have a credit available, **1 credit = 1 live room (one match)**. It costs you no coins and you can start straight away.
2. **Coins:** 20 coins for 4 participants or 30 coins for 6 participants. You can pay with your own coins, or ask your audience to contribute coins while the match is being set up. If your wallet has less than the full cost, all your available coins are used and the match waits until the rest is filled.
The number of matches you can start with coins is your coin balance divided by the cost. For example, 100 coins is 5 matches of 4 participants (20 each) or 3 matches of 6 participants (30 each). Only one match can run at a time.
Credits and coins are for the **broadcaster only**. **Participants join for free** whichever way you start.

### B4. Can my audience help fund the match?
**Audience:** Broadcaster, Participant
Yes, but only for matches started with coins. While the pool is not full, any viewer in your room can add Gold Coins to fill it. A contribution is capped at what is still missing. Once the pool is full the match opens for joining, and an announcement is sent app-wide: "{Broadcaster name} is starting New Tug of War. 🤑 Participate & Win rewards."
Matches started with a free credit cannot take contributions.

### B5. How many free credits do I get?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
Free credits let you start a match without spending coins. Your credits are a **weekly total**, reviewed every week, and depend on your Health Heart:
- 🟢 Green: **3 credits**
- 🟡 Yellow: **2 credits**
- 🔴 Red: **1 credit**
When your credits for the week are used up you will see "No credits left for today". You can still start a match with coins, or wait for the Monday refresh.
The app shows your remaining credits as a counter, for example **2/3 Credits Available**.

### B6. Can I run two matches at once?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
No. If you already have a match from today that is not finished or cancelled, you will see **"Cannot Initialise New Tug Of War"**. Finish or cancel the current one first.

### B7. What happens if my stream ends mid-match? Can I resume?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
If your live ends while the match is in the matching stage, the match is **paused**. When you resume it, all registered participants get a notification: "Tug of War Resumed – TOW is resumed by {Broadcaster name}."

### B8. Can I cancel a match? Do I get my credit or coins back?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
You can cancel only before pairing starts, meaning while the match is in setup or joining. After that you will see **"Cannot end at this point"**.
On cancel:
- Credit-funded match: your free credit is returned.
- Coin-funded match: all coins in the pool are returned to your wallet.
Cancelled matches pay no prizes.

### B9. What is the daily 4-match goal?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
The daily goal is **4 valid Tug of War matches per day** (midnight to midnight IST). The progress text reads: "You have completed X no of Tug Of War out of 4."
- A match counts only if it reached **Completed** and was valid. Cancelled, aborted or ended-early matches do not count.
- A match is not valid if it was ended for rule infractions, suspected vote collusion or zero voter engagement. It also does not count if you left the room before the final results screen.
- A match finishing after 11:59:59 PM IST counts for the next day.
- The broadcaster's 9 coins for each match are added to your daily live target (see B12).

### B11. Where can I see my weekly Tug of War performance?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
Your Profile's Tug of War section shows your weekly and monthly status, your Health Heart, and whether any of your videos were marked "Rejected". Weekly figures include matches completed, recordings made and recordings rejected this week. See B13 for how the weekly review works.

### B12. What does the broadcaster earn from a match?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
The broadcaster earns **9 Gold Coins** after the winners of the room are revealed. These 9 coins are **added to your daily live target**. They are **not shown in your coin balance**. A broadcaster's coins increase through **gifting** from the audience.

### B13. How does the weekly review work?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
Your Health Heart and weekly free credits follow a weekly cycle:
- **Monday to Saturday:** post high-quality recordings of your Tug of War moments. Posting is mandatory: it proves authentic participation, keeps your Health Heart active, and qualifies you for next week's credit refresh.
- **Weekend:** an official review checks each recording for audio quality, camera presence, singing effort and community guidelines.
- **Monday:** your credits are refreshed for the week based on your performance: 3 credits for high quality (Green), 2 for average (Yellow), 1 for low or no activity (Red).

### B14. Why was my recording rejected, and why did I get fewer credits?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
A recording can be rejected in the weekend review for offensive content, copyright strikes, or silent or fake singing. Rejected recordings, poor audio or video quality, and inactivity pull your Health Heart down toward Yellow or Red, which means fewer free credits on Monday (see B5).
To recover: post clear, high-quality, compliant singing recordings, avoid background noise, blank screens and non-musical broadcasts, make sure none of your recordings is marked "Rejected", and stay active from Monday to Saturday.

## PART C: PARTICIPANT

### C1. Do I pay to join a match?
**Audience:** Participant
No. Joining is free. The match is paid for by the broadcaster, using a free credit or coins (the broadcaster's own, or contributed by the audience). You need an open seat and you can't join the same match twice.

### C2. What if the match is full or not open?
**Audience:** Participant
- "War is not accepting participants": the match is not in the joining stage.
- "User already joined this war": you are already in.
- "War is full": all seats are taken.

### C3. Can I take over an empty seat during the match?
**Audience:** Participant
Yes. If a participant leaves their seat during pairing, any viewer can claim the empty seat. If someone else took it first you will see "Seat Already Acquired."

### C4. What happens if I leave or get disconnected?
**Audience:** Participant
- Waiting in the lobby: you are removed from the match.
- Assigned to a seat but not yet singing: your seat is freed and another waiting participant is picked.
- While singing: your turn ends immediately and is marked **faulty** (see C7).
If you come back and a seat is open, you are put back into it automatically.

### C5. How does a duel and voting work?
**Audience:** Participant
Each pair sings in turn. When both have finished, the audience has **15 seconds** to vote, one vote per viewer per pair. The one with more votes wins.
Messages you may see:
- "User must have completed their task to be eligible for voting"
- "Voting window has closed" / "Voting window closed"
- "User has already voted"
- "Vote successfully registered"
- "User not assigned to this seat"

### C6. How much do I win?
**Audience:** Participant
Per pair: the **Winner gets 8 Gold Coins** and the **Loser gets 2 Gold Coins**.

### C7. What does "faulty" mean and what does it cost me?
**Audience:** Participant
You are marked faulty if you leave the room while singing or your turn is reported as faulty. In some matches, if **2 or more** participants are marked faulty, every faulty participant does not receive rewards.

### C8. When do I get my reward?
**Audience:** Participant
Rewards are **credited to your account automatically** after the result is revealed. It may take some time to reflect in your balance.
Coins are paid only when a match is completed normally. If the stream disconnects or ends early (an aborted or incomplete match), no coins are awarded.

### C9. Do I need to record and post my Reveal Time performance?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
During the **Reveal Time**, the broadcaster sings a song while revealing the winners one by one. Start recording when you begin singing and revealing, and keep recording through the reveal. Post the recording to your profile. Posting is mandatory: it proves authentic participation, keeps your Health Heart active and qualifies you for next week's credits. The recording is also used in the weekly review.

### C10. What is the Health Heart (Green / Yellow / Red)?
**Audience:** Broadcaster
The Health Heart is the **broadcaster's** standing. It is shown on your profile and in the match lobby, and it sets the weekly free credits:
- 🟢 Green (good standing, 3 credits): high-quality performances, active singing, and no rejected recordings.
- 🟡 Yellow (needs improvement, 2 credits): occasional low activity, poor audio or video quality, or missed recordings.
- 🔴 Red (at risk, 1 credit): multiple rejected recordings, policy violations, or long inactivity.
To recover, post clear, high-quality, compliant singing recordings, avoid background noise, blank screens and non-musical broadcasts, and make sure none is marked "Rejected". See B13 and B14.

### C11. How can I claim free Gold Coins in RoxStar?
**Audience:** Participant
If you have won Tug of War, free Gold Coins will be credited to you; they will appear in your account after a short time.

## PART D: LEADERBOARDS

### D1. Today's leaderboard
**Audience:** Everyone
Shows participants who earned **more than 0** Tug of War coins today (IST). Ranked by total coins. The summary shows the top 50; the full list shows 20 per page. Each row shows rank, name, avatar, level badge, VIP tag and frame, and family badge. Messages: "No participants found for today", "Page number exceeds total pages".

### D2. Monthly winners leaderboard
**Audience:** Everyone
Ranks users by total coins won this calendar month and shows total match wins. Resets on the 1st at midnight IST. Empty message: "No winners found for this month".
