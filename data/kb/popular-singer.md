## PART A: OVERVIEW

### A1. What is the Popular Singer Event and what rewards are given?
The Popular Singer Event is a scheduled singing competition hosted in eligible party rooms. Participants sing one by one, room users vote for their favorite performers, and the singer with the highest votes wins. The winner receives the exclusive Popular Singer Tag and Chat Bubble.

### A2. How can a user get the Popular Singer Tag, and is there a Verified Singer badge?
The Popular Singer Tag is earned exclusively by winning an official Popular Singer Contest. A "Verified Singer" badge does not currently exist on the platform.

### A3. Why is the Chat Bubble reward not showing in my tools after winning?
The Chat Bubble is automatically activated directly on your chat messages once the tag is awarded. It does not appear as an inventory item in your tools menu.

### A4. How do users renew or save their Popular Singer Tag?
Existing tag holders must complete at least 100 points within each renewal cycle to keep their tag. If a user earns 100 points, the tag renews automatically. If they fail to reach 100 points, the tag is removed.

### A5. What activities award points toward renewing the Popular Singer Tag?
Points are earned by completing these activities during the cycle:
- Audio cover song post: **25 points**
- Audio duet song post: **30 points**
- Video duet song post: **35 points**
- Popular Singer room performance: **50 points**
- Getting featured on the Popular Page: **100 points**

### A6. How can a user reactivate a removed Popular Singer Tag?
If a user's tag has been removed, earning 100 points is not enough. The user must complete all 5 required tasks totaling 240 points:
1. Audio cover song post (25 pts)
2. Audio duet song post (30 pts)
3. Video duet song post (35 pts)
4. Popular Singer room performance (50 pts)
5. Featured on the Popular Page (100 pts)
Completing all 5 tasks (240 points) reactivates the tag.

### A7. What happens to the Popular Singer Tag if a user leaves their family?
If a user earned their tag through family room quotas and leaves that family, the tag is removed. It can only be restored if the user provides an official No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their previous family captain.

## PART B: ROOM ELIGIBILITY AND SCHEDULING

### B1. What criteria make a party room eligible to host a Popular Singer Contest?
A party room becomes eligible by completing room task and gifting targets:
- **5,000 points target:** Grants 1 contest opportunity.
- **10,000 or 20,000 points target:** Grants recurring contest opportunities.
Once eligible, the scheduling option appears on the Popular Singer event page.

### B2. How often can an eligible room host a Popular Singer Contest?
Each eligible party room can host a maximum of **1 contest per week**. Once a contest is held, the room must wait until the following week to host again.

### B3. How and when should a room owner schedule the contest?
Room owners schedule contests through the banner on the Popular Singer page by picking a date and time. Contests must be scheduled at least **3 to 5 hours in advance**. Same-day scheduling requests made less than 1 hour before cannot be accepted.

### B4. What room settings are required during a scheduled contest?
The party room must remain fully unlocked and set to Singing/Performance mode during the scheduled session. Locked rooms or chat-mode rooms cannot host the event.

### B5. When does the Popular Singer Contest take place in the Official Room?
The Official Room contest takes place exclusively every Sunday (e.g. 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM).

### B6. What happens if a contest is postponed due to technical issues?
If technical issues occur, the contest is officially postponed and rescheduled. Once technical teams resolve the issue, room owners are notified to reschedule their room on the event page.

## PART C: PARTICIPATION AND QUEUE

### C1. Who is eligible to participate in the singing competition?
Any registered user can join the singing queue and perform. However, users who already hold an active Popular Singer Tag cannot compete again.

### C2. Can a user participate using multiple IDs?
No. Participating with multiple IDs from the same person is strictly prohibited and leads to immediate disqualification. Each user must participate using only one account.

### C3. How is the singing queue managed, and what is the mic time limit?
Participants perform one by one strictly in queue order. Manual queue shifting or skipping is disabled. Each singer has a maximum limit of **3 to 4 minutes on mic**. After this limit, the queue advances to the next participant.

## PART D: VOTING AND WINNER SELECTION

### D1. How does the voting process work?
When the contest begins, a blinking Hand Voting icon appears in the room. Once a singer completes their 3-minute performance, voting buttons activate for all users present in the room to cast their votes.

### D2. How does 3x Bonus Voting work and when does it apply?
3x Bonus Voting applies exclusively during active official room contest sessions. Gifts sent outside the official session, in private inboxes, or on posted songs count at the standard 1x rate.

### D3. When does voting close?
**Audience:** Everyone
Voting for the Popular Singer contest ends at 8:00 PM. No votes are accepted after that time.

### D4. Who is declared the winner in the popular singer contest?
**Audience:** Everyone
The singer with the highest total vote count is declared the winner and receives the exclusive Popular Singer Tag and Chat Bubble.
