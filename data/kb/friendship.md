## PART A: OVERVIEW

### A1. What is the Friendship Feature and what bond types are available?
The Friendship Feature allows users to build relationship bonds, complete daily shared activities, earn Friendship EXP and Familiarity Points, certify relationships, level up, and unlock exclusive rewards. The four bond types are: **Couple**, **Bestie**, **Buddy**, and **Confidant**.

### A2. What are the relationship limits, and can I add more partner slots?
Each user has default limits:
- **Couple:** Maximum 1
- **Bestie:** Maximum 3
- **Buddy:** Maximum 3
- **Confidant:** Maximum 3
Users can have up to 10 total partners. You can increase slots for Bestie, Buddy, or Confidant by spending Gold Coins. The Couple limit is strictly fixed at 1 bond per user.

### A3. How do relationship display and privacy settings work?
Certified friendship bonds and partner names are displayed on user profiles. Users can control visibility through privacy settings; disabling relationship privacy hides relationship information from other users.

### A4. What happens if a friendship relationship is terminated?
Users can terminate or break an existing relationship. A **10-day recovery grace period** applies following termination, during which the relationship can be restored without losing points. If not restored after the 10 days, Familiarity Points begin decaying at a rate of **10 points per day**.

## PART B: FAMILIARITY TASKS AND EXP

### B1. How do users earn points through the Chat Task?
Chatting together in private messages awards **10 EXP per eligible interaction**, up to a maximum daily limit of **100 EXP**.

### B2. How do users earn points through the Duet Join Task?
Performing a duet together awards **70 EXP to each user**, contributing a total of **140 EXP across the pair**.

### B3. How do users earn points through the Room Seat Task?
Sitting together on eligible paired seats in a party room (such as seats 1&2, 2&3, 4&6, or 7&8) awards **10 EXP per minute**, up to a maximum daily limit of **100 EXP**.

### B4. How do users earn points through the Gift Task?
Sending Gold Coin gifts or eligible free/silver gifts contributes toward friendship progress, typically counting 100 to 200 worth of gift progress per day based on task rules.

### B5. Where do Room EXP Cards and Family EXP Rewards reflect?
Room EXP Cards and Family EXP won from event reward boxes do not add to daily reset counters. They are credited to the user's Baggage and contribute directly to the lifetime/total room or family EXP.

## PART C: RELATIONSHIP CERTIFICATION

### C1. How do users certify their relationship?
Once a pair reaches **1,000 Familiarity Points**, they become eligible to certify their relationship. They choose their preferred bond type (Couple, Bestie, Buddy, or Confidant) and complete the certification in the app.

### C2. How do Event Certificates (like Jodi No. 1) work and when are they issued?
Event relationship certificates are issued on the event profile page following official verification of completed event tasks and songs. If an event certificate fails to load or cannot be downloaded, please provide your User ID, and we will get back to you with the updated download link.

## PART D: LEVELS AND REWARDS

### D1. What rewards and benefits are unlocked across the 7 Friendship Levels?
There are 7 Friendship Levels (Level 1 to Level 7). Earning EXP unlocks exclusive benefits:
- **Frames:** Special relationship frames unlocked at designated levels (including Levels 1, 4, 6, and 7).
- **Badges:** Relationship badges displayed on profiles and in chat.
- **Tail Lights:** Exclusive visual entry effects and animations.
- **Profile Linking:** Options to display linked relationship status on profiles.

### D2. What prizes are given to winning pairs in Friendship / Jodi events?
Top winning pairs receive Gold Coin prizes (divided equally between partners) and Customized Frames. All registered participants receive an exclusive participation frame.

### D3. How are Customized Partner Frames personalized, and when are they delivered?
Each partner provides one custom word (up to 7 letters) to display on their frame, typically featuring their partner's name. Event frames are dispatched directly to eligible users' Baggage and avatar settings once official results are verified.
