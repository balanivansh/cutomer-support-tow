## PART A: OVERVIEW

### A1. What is PK Battle?
PK Battle is a real-time, head-to-head competition where a broadcaster invites another broadcaster to compete live. Viewers send gifts and tickets during a countdown timer to boost their broadcaster's score. When the timer ends, the broadcaster with the higher score wins.

### A2. How does a PK Battle match run step by step?
1. **Invite:** The broadcaster selects PK Battle and invites an opponent.
2. **Accept:** The opponent accepts the invitation.
3. **Battle:** Both broadcasters compete while viewers send gifts to increase their score.
4. **Result:** When the timer ends, scores are compared and the winner is announced on screen.
5. **Next:** Broadcasters can start another battle or continue their solo stream.

### A3. How does participant voice interaction work during PK?
During an active PK Battle, room participants can speak directly to the host. However, participant voices are isolated and will not be audible to the opponent broadcaster or other viewers in the room.

### A4. What EXP rewards are earned from PK Battle wins?
Winning 3 PK battles awards 400 EXP, up to a maximum daily limit of 400 EXP.

### A5. Why is the PK Battle option missing or disabled on my Live stream?
The PK Battle option and Tug of War are disabled if your live broadcast is still in its initial Audition Period. During the Audition Period:
- You cannot send PK battle invitations.
- You cannot host Tug of War games.
- Your live streaming is limited to 2 hours per day.

### A6. How does a broadcaster get Final Approval to unlock PK and Tug of War?
To complete the Audition Period and get Final Approval:
1. Record and post 3 live singing video covers directly during your live broadcast sessions.
2. Submit the video covers for official review.
Once approved, full live verification is granted and all features (PK, Tug of War, Piggy Bank, and unlimited live hours) unlock automatically.

### A7. How can I check if my live video recordings are approved or rejected?
Check your live broadcast screen to see the status of your submitted video covers: it will show either Approved or Rejected. Once approved, the PK Battle option and Piggy Bank activate immediately.

## PART B: INVITATION AND MATCHMAKING

### B1. Why is the Send invitation option for PK Battles not visible?
The Send invitation option for PK Battles is not available at all times. It is enabled only when your room is in a solo exclusive session, and during designated official battle hours at night.

### B2. What should I do if PK is accepted but fails to connect or disconnects the live stream?
Restart the app and check your network connection. If your live stream repeatedly disconnects or drops when starting a PK battle, please provide your device model and User ID, and we will look into it and get back to you.

### B3. Can room hosts block opponent Toli members during PK battle hours?
No. The room blocking system is disabled for all Toli members during the 9:00 PM – 10:00 PM battle window. To ensure fair competition, no host or user can block an opponent member from their room during active battle hours.

## PART C: SCORING AND GIFTS

### C1. What is the daily limit for free tickets in PK Battles?
Each broadcaster can receive a maximum of 50 free platform-sponsored tickets per day. Free tickets are provided by the platform to support broadcasters. Once the 50 free tickets are consumed, supporters can continue sending regular paid gifts without any limit.

### C2. Why are free tickets not working from unverified accounts?
Free platform-sponsored tickets can only be sent from Verified user accounts. This restriction is enforced to prevent fake account exploitation and abuse of free benefits.

### C3. Where and when do battle gifts like Pichkari appear?
Event battle gifts (such as Pichkari) only appear and can be sent in Solo Singing Rooms during exclusive sessions and active live battles. They do not appear in regular party rooms or outside official battle times.

### C4. Can family members support another Toli during battle hours?
No. During the 8:00 PM – 10:00 PM battle window, users cannot send gifts or support another family's Toli group. Supporting a different Toli is blocked to ensure fair competition. Cross-room support is only permitted in solo exclusive rooms outside battle hours.

### C5. What should I do if the PK battle win count is not updating on my profile?
PK match counts can take 15 to 30 minutes to synchronize across servers. Please restart the app and check your profile again. If the battle count still does not update after 1 hour, please provide your User ID and your opponent's User ID, and we will get back to you.

## PART D: AUDIO AND CONDUCT RULES

### D1. Why does voice echo occur during PK battles, and how can it be fixed?
Voice echo during PK battles or two-way live calls is caused by acoustic feedback when broadcasting on open device speakerphones without headphones. Both broadcasters and callers should use earphones or headsets to eliminate echo loops.

### D2. Can a broadcaster mute the opponent during a PK battle?
Independent muting of the opponent without muting your own stream is not currently supported and is under review by our product development team.

### D3. What are the penalties for abusive language or harassment during battles?
The platform enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against abusive language, harassment, and misconduct. Violating groups or rooms face immediate event disqualification, forfeiture of all rewards, and repeated offenses result in permanent account suspension and family dissolution.
